Privacy notice

1. Summary

Lagerverwaltung stores all data exclusively on your device. The app provider does not collect, receive or store any personal data.

The app requires no user account and contains no advertising, no tracking, no analytics tools and no third-party software. The app itself does not connect to the internet.

3. Who is responsible for the data in the app?

Whoever uses the app (for example a practice, an institution or a company) decides which data to enter and is the controller of that data within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR. The provider has no access to this data and is neither controller nor processor of it.

4. Which data does the app process?

  • Inventory data: storages, sub-storages, items, item numbers, batch numbers, expiry dates, quantities, target stock and consumption records. This data normally does not relate to a person.
  • Company details (optional): name and address. They appear in the PDF report.
  • Change log: for every change the app stores the time and the kind of change. If you enter a user name in the settings, it is recorded as well. The device name is only stored if you switch this on in the settings (off by default). User and device names may relate to a person.
  • Report archive: copies of the PDF reports created, with document ID and checksum.
  • Backups: copies of all data as a file, optionally encrypted with a password.
  • App settings, such as language and appearance.

Please do not enter patient data or other sensitive personal data into the app, not even in free-text fields such as “Note” or “Action / remark”. The app is not intended for this.

5. Access to device features

  • Camera: only for scanning barcodes and QR codes. No images are stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications: only if you switch on the daily reminder. Reminders are created locally on the device.
  • Files: import, export and backups only access files that you choose or save yourself.

6. Disclosure and transfer

The app does not pass data on to third parties and does not send data to any server. There are no automated decisions and no profiling.

7. Storage period and deletion

The data stays on your device until you delete it. The app offers: deleting individual items, batches, storages and sub-storages; adjustable automatic deletion of old consumption data and log entries; deleting the change log and the report archive; and “Delete all data permanently” (Mac: Settings › Privacy; iPhone/iPad: Settings › Privacy & Retention), which also removes backups in the app area, company details and settings (but not backups in a backup folder you chose yourself).

When you uninstall the app, its data is normally removed from the device. On macOS, files may remain in your user folder; use “Delete all data permanently” first.

Files that you export or share from the app (CSV, PDF, backups) are outside the app and must be deleted by you.

8. Data security

The app stores data in the protected app area of your device. Protection depends largely on your device: device lock, passcode or login password and disk encryption (FileVault or iOS data protection). The app has no login of its own; anyone with access to the unlocked device can view the data.

Backups that you save with a password are encrypted with AES-256-GCM (key derivation with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). The password is not stored and cannot be recovered by the provider. Automatic daily backups are stored unencrypted in the app area and are protected only by device encryption; you can switch them off in the “Backup” dialog. If you additionally choose a backup folder outside the app there, the daily backups are also stored in that folder and survive deleting the app; they can optionally be encrypted. The password chosen for this is stored in your device’s keychain. You must delete files in the backup folder yourself if needed.

9. Apple and your device

When you obtain the app, Apple processes data (for example Apple ID and payment data) as an independent controller; the provider has no influence on this. If you allow sharing of analytics and crash data with app developers in your device settings, the provider may receive technical crash and usage statistics through Apple.

If you use iCloud Backup or Time Machine, the app’s data folder on your device may be included in those backups. This is your responsibility.

10. Contacting the provider

If you write to the provider, your details (for example email address and message content) are processed to handle your request on the basis of Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR and deleted once the request is resolved and no statutory retention duties apply. Please do not send data from the app that is not needed to handle your request.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

For data in the app: the provider has no access to it. Please contact the operator (controller) of the app data, for example your employer. The app supports access and portability (export as CSV, PDF or backup) and erasure (see section 7).

12. Notes for operators (controllers)

If you use the app in an institution or company, you are the controller. Please note in particular:

  • Keep a record of processing activities where required (Art. 30 GDPR).
  • Inform employees that the change log contains user names and, where enabled, device names (Art. 13 GDPR; for employee data also national employment-data rules, in Germany § 26 BDSG).
  • Protect devices (device lock, disk encryption, current system updates) and control access to backups and exports (technical and organisational measures, Art. 32 GDPR).
  • Define retention and deletion periods and apply them in the settings, taking statutory documentation and retention duties into account.
  • Do not record patient data in the app.

13. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice if the app or the legal situation changes, for example if iCloud synchronisation is added later. The current version is available in the app under Settings (Mac: “Privacy” tab; iPhone/iPad: Privacy & Retention).
